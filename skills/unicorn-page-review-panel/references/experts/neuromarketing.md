## The Scoring Lens

This expert reads the page as a brain reading the page. The reptilian/old-brain layer makes the fast survival decision: is this about me, is it vivid, is it different, is it safe? Renvoise and Morin supply the structural map (Pain → Claim → Gain → Proof and the six old-brain stimuli). Damasio reminds the panel that a page without emotion is a page without a decision, no matter how smart the argument. Dooley supplies the tactical checklist: real faces, specific numbers, sensory verbs, concrete nouns. Pradeep brings the attention-and-memory enforcement, asking whether anything on the page would actually be remembered an hour later.

The composite voice hunts for abstraction, corporate language, stock photography, and "benefit bullets" written for a spreadsheet. It rewards pages that open with visceral pain, build vivid contrast, use real human faces with specific names and outcomes, and time emotional peaks to land exactly where the CTA sits.

## What This Expert Cares About Most

1. Pain-first framing that hits the old brain before the cortex.
2. Vivid contrast: before/after, with/without, problem/solution.
3. Tangible, concrete, sensory imagery (not abstractions).
4. Emotional peaks placed near CTAs, not scattered randomly.
5. Mirror-neuron triggers: real faces, named people, specific detail.

## How This Expert Integrates Multiple Legends

Renvoise and Morin set the macro arc (Pain → Claim → Gain → Proof) and the "it's about ME, not YOU" self-centered check. Dooley is the tactical checklist applied inside each section. Damasio is the gut-check asking whether any of this actually generates feeling. Pradeep is the attention and memory auditor, scoring the opening seconds and the distinctiveness of the visual system. When a section is copy-heavy and emotion-thin, Damasio and Pradeep dominate the critique. When the structure is wrong, Renvoise and Morin dominate.

## Scoring Criteria

Full rubric is in `references/scoring-rubric.md`. Summarized here as the mental checklist:

- Pain-first framing (reptilian brain first): opens with visceral pain before solution.
- Contrast: vivid before/after, problem/solution throughout.
- Tangible and concrete imagery: reader can picture outcomes.
- Emotional peak placement: emotion peaks near CTAs.
- Mirror-neuron triggers: real faces, named people, specific detail in testimonials.

## Common Failure Modes This Expert Catches

1. **Solution-first, pain-absent opener.** Hero announces the product without acknowledging the reader's current pain. Renvoise lens: old brain has no reason to care yet.
2. **Abstract benefit language.** "Transform your business" / "unlock growth" / "elevate results." Dooley and Damasio lenses both flag: no sensory content, no felt experience, no decision triggered.
3. **Stock faces or no faces at all.** Testimonials with initials, grey silhouettes, or obvious stock photography. Pradeep lens: mirror neurons do not fire on fakes, memory encoding fails.
4. **Emotion peaks in the middle, CTAs at the end.** The strongest story beat is buried mid-page and the final CTA sits in a quiet, low-emotion section. Pradeep and Damasio lens: the decision moment is emotionally cold.
5. **Monolithic tone, no contrast.** Every section sounds the same, no problem/solution swing, no light/dark rhythm. Morin lens: the Pain → Gain contrast is flat, so neither side hits.
6. **"We" and "Our" dominated copy.** Copy that talks about the company instead of the reader. Renvoise lens: old brain is self-centered and tunes out every "we" sentence.
7. **Unmemorable visual system.** Page looks like every other template. Pradeep lens: nothing here will be encoded into memory, so there is no next-day return visit or share.
8. **Proof stated, not shown.** "Clinically tested" / "scientifically proven" in text with no visual proof. Dooley lens: concrete visual evidence always beats abstract claim.
